Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard — Detailed Project Report
1. Introduction

This project analyzes retail sales data using Power BI to understand business performance, customer behavior, and product demand. The analysis focuses on identifying key drivers of revenue and uncovering insights that can support better business decision-making.

The main objective of the project is to analyze historical sales data and provide insights that can help the company increase sales by 15% in the next three months while minimizing financial losses from inefficient inventory management or poorly performing products.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow including data cleaning, transformation, exploratory analysis, visualization, and business recommendations.

2. Problem Statement

Retail businesses need to understand which factors influence their sales performance in order to grow effectively. Without proper analysis, companies may invest in products or strategies that do not generate sufficient revenue.

The goal of this project is:

Increase sales by 15% in the next three months without wasting cash on bad stock.

To address this problem, the analysis focuses on identifying:
The most profitable product categories
The most valuable customer segments
The best-performing sales channels
Seasonal trends in sales performance
Customer purchasing behavior

Gender

Product ID

Quantity
Discount

Store Name

Store Size

Feature Engineering

Additional fields were created to support analysis:

Age groups derived from customer age
Year extracted from transaction date
Revenue calculated using DAX
Revenue was calculated using the formula:
Revenue = Quantity × List Price × (1 − Discount)
This formula helps calculate the actual revenue after applying discounts.

6. Key Metrics Used

Several key metrics were created using DAX to analyze business performance.
Total Revenue
Measures the total revenue generated from product sales.
Total Orders
Represents the number of transactions completed.
Total Quantity Sold

Represents the total number of products sold across all transactions.

Average Order Value (AOV)
Measures the average amount spent per transaction.

Formula:

AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders

A higher AOV indicates customers are purchasing more items or higher-priced products per order.

8. Key Insights
Store Channel Performance
Physical stores generate approximately 80% of total revenue, while online stores contribute only about 20%.
This suggests that the majority of customers prefer shopping in physical stores rather than online platforms.
